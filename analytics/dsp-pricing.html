Analysis summary
Avg price per DSP
Bars = volume-weighted avg $/pkg for each DSP in the current selection. Dashed line = selection average. Hover a bar for its daily volume.
DSP × station price matrix
Volume-weighted avg $/pkg per DSP in each station where it operates; darker teal = higher price. Overall combines a DSP's station cells weighted by each station's daily volume share of the DSSLC region (normalized over the stations that DSP serves). Vol = daily pkg volume attributed to the DSP in that station.
Method. Chart & KPIs: for each DSP, avg price = Σ(ZIP price × DSP volume in that ZIP) ÷ Σ(DSP volume), over priced ZIPs in the current selection. Where a DSP has zero recorded volume (several DSSLC stations have no daily-volume column in the source data), the plain average of its ZIP prices is used instead so the DSP still appears. Matrix: each cell is the DSP's volume-weighted avg within that station; a DSP's Overall value combines its station cells weighted by each station's daily-volume share of the DSSLC region, normalized over the stations it operates in. The Station avg row is that station's volume-weighted avg over all its priced ZIPs, and the region Overall is Σ(station avg × station volume share).
